## Idea
AI-Powered Civic Grievance Platform: This initiative enables the citizens to submit complaints related to any civic issues like potholes, garbage, etc. in a very simple way by capturing a picture and writing down a small description about the issue. It is AI which identifies the kind of issue and its severity. When several people complain about the same civic issue, the initiative clubbed all those complaints together with the number of people affected. This complaint is forwarded to the concerned department and the citizen gets to know about the status of the complaint on a live public dashboard. What makes this initiative different from other initiatives already in place is the requirement of the officer to provide a post-complaint photograph in case he mentions the complaint as “Resolved", AI then compares the before and after photos to verify that the work was actually done, preventing fake resolutions and building real trust between citizens and the government.
